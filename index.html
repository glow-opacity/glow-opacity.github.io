How the opacities were calculated

The underlying monochromatic opacities were calculated with STAR (STA-Revised), an atomic code based on the Super-Transition-Array method for plasmas in local thermodynamic equilibrium. The STA approach statistically represents the immense number of atomic configurations and transitions in a tractable form; GLOW then integrates those spectra into 1024 energy groups and builds the Planck- and Rosseland-weighted quantities served here.

Method reference Krief, Feigel & Gazit (2016) “Solar Opacity Calculations Using the Super-Transition-Array Method” · ApJ 821, 45

Mixture definition

Solar composition

The 1 Z⊙ table uses a normalized 15-element subset of the photospheric abundances compiled by Asplund, Amarsi & Grevesse (2021; AAG21). These are the number fractions supplied to STAR.

For the 0.1 and 10 Z⊙ mixtures, hydrogen and helium remain unscaled, every heavier element is multiplied by 0.1 or 10, and the complete 15-element vector is renormalized.

Composition reference Asplund, Amarsi & Grevesse (2021) “The chemical make-up of the Sun: A 2020 vision” · A&A 653, A141

i

Published 1024-group values are treated as piecewise constant inside each native group. Custom boundaries integrate the physical weight only over the overlapping interval. Extrapolation outside the published temperature, density, and energy ranges is disabled.

i

Each group value is the exact integral of STAR's tabulated spectrum, linear between STAR's frequency points, over the group. The groups reproduce STAR's grey Planck and Rosseland means. At low density many spectral lines are narrower than STAR's frequency grid. STAR's grey Planck mean then sums the line strengths directly, and only the line part of the groups is rescaled to that sum. Values whose line content is uncertain carry a quality flag, shown in the preview, the downloads and as hollow plot markers: lines_aliased (line strength rescaled by more than 10%, and lines_aliased_x2 beyond a factor of 2), lines_unresolved (the groups hold the continuum only; the line contribution is included in the grey Planck mean), rosseland_lines_rescaled (line blocking per group approximate below about 20 eV), sub_cell (group narrower than STAR's frequency spacing), and grey_rescaled (all groups of the cell scaled by 2–30% to reproduce STAR's grey mean).
